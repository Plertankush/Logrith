Questions

What is Logrith?

Logrith is an independent publication about technology, startups and digital business, with a library of free browser-based tools alongside the writing.

Are the tools really free?

Yes. Every tool is free, needs no account and runs in your browser. The site is supported by advertising.

Do my files get uploaded?

The image, PDF and text tools process files inside your browser, so documents stay on your device. Each tool page explains how that tool handles data.

Who writes Logrith?

Logrith is built and published by Plert Ankush, an independent founder. You can read more on the founder page.
