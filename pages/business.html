How Each Partnership Type Works

Advertising

Logrith's tools are used by people actively doing a specific task — compressing a file, calculating a number, generating a password — which makes placements contextual rather than interruptive. We keep ad density low on purpose, so any advertising partnership is evaluated for fit with the page it would appear on, not just budget.

Integration

If your product solves a problem adjacent to what Logrith already does — an API, a data source, a specialized conversion — we're open to discussing how it could power a new tool or improve an existing one. These conversations usually start with a short technical overview of what you're proposing and why it's a good fit for a browser-first, no-login platform.

Strategic Partnership

This covers longer-term collaborations: co-authored content, link exchanges with genuinely relevant sites, or joint development of a new tool or feature. We're a small, independently-run platform, so strategic partnerships are evaluated case by case rather than through a formal program.

Frequently Asked Questions

What information should I include in a proposal?

What you're proposing, what you're offering in return, a rough timeline, and a budget range if advertising or a paid arrangement is involved. The more specific the proposal, the faster we can give you a real answer rather than a generic reply.

Do you accept sponsored tool placements or paid rankings?

No. Tool visibility on Logrith is based on usefulness and category fit, not payment. We're open to advertising placements that are clearly labeled as such, but we don't let sponsorship influence which tools get built or how they're ranked.

How long does a typical partnership discussion take?

The initial reply comes within 24 hours. From there, it depends on the complexity of the proposal — a simple advertising placement can move quickly, while an integration or strategic partnership usually needs a follow-up call or email thread to work out details.
