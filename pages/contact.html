Before You Write In

Do I need to create an account to contact you?

No. The form on this page doesn't require a login, and we don't ask for an account when you email us directly. Just send your message and we'll reply to the email address you provide.

What should I include in a bug report?

The name of the tool, what you were trying to do, what actually happened, and your browser and device if you know them (for example, "Chrome on Android"). A screenshot helps but isn't required — a clear description is usually enough for us to reproduce the issue.

How do you decide which tool suggestions to build?

We track every suggestion that comes in through this form. Tools that get requested more than once, or that fill an obvious gap in an existing category, tend to move up the list first. There's no guaranteed timeline, but nothing submitted is ignored.

Is this page the right place for a data or privacy question?

Yes, general privacy questions are welcome here. For the full details on how Logrith handles data, see the Privacy Policy first — most questions are already answered there.

I sent a message but haven't heard back — what now?

We aim to reply within 24 hours on business days. If it's been longer than that, check your spam folder first, then try emailing hello@logrith.in directly in case the original message didn't go through.
