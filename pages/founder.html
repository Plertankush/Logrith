Why I Built Logrith

I kept running into the same frustration: simple digital tasks — compressing an image, converting a file, calculating something quick — were locked behind subscriptions, intrusive ads, or slow, bloated websites.

Logrith is my answer to that: a single place with genuinely free, fast, private tools that don't ask for your email, your file uploads, or your money to do basic digital work. No subscriptions. No hidden catches. Just tools that work.

My long-term goal with Logrith isn't to chase every possible feature or compete head-on with giant, well-funded platforms. It's to build something genuinely useful, keep it honest and sustainable, and make it the default choice for anyone who values their privacy and time.

A note on how this site is built: Logrith's tools, blog content, and SEO are developed with AI assistance as a core part of the process, directed and reviewed by me. I'm transparent about this because I believe it's the honest thing to do.
