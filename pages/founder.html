Who I Am

I'm Ankush Maurya, a solo founder and builder. Online, across every platform I use, I go by Plert Ankush — the same identity on every account, so anyone who finds me on one platform can trust it's genuinely the same person everywhere else.

I'm not a traditional software engineer with a formal coding background. I'm a founder who learned to build by doing, and who now builds entire products using AI as my core development process — a practice often called "vibecoding." Logrith, every tool on it, every line of code behind it, and every piece of content on this site, including this page, was built this way: through direction, iteration, and collaboration with AI systems, not hand-written line by line in the traditional sense.

I think this is worth stating plainly rather than hiding. Logrith is a real, working, free product used by real people — and it was built by one person directing AI tools to do it, start to finish. I believe this is an honest and increasingly common way products get built today, and I'd rather be transparent about it than pretend otherwise.

Why I Built Logrith

I kept running into the same frustration: simple digital tasks — compressing an image, converting a file, calculating something quick — were locked behind subscriptions, intrusive ads, or sites that uploaded your files to unknown servers just to do something that could run entirely in a browser. It felt unnecessary and, often, exploitative of people who just needed something simple done.

Logrith is my answer to that: a single place with genuinely free, fast, private tools that don't ask for your email, your file uploads, or your money to do basic digital work. No subscriptions, no dark patterns, no unnecessary data collection. Just tools that work.

My long-term goal with Logrith isn't to chase every possible feature or compete head-on with giant, well-funded platforms. It's to build something genuinely useful, keep it honest and sustainable through simple, non-intrusive advertising, and let the quality of the tools speak for themselves.

A note on how this site is built: Logrith's tools, blog content, and SEO are developed with AI assistance as a core part of the process, directed and reviewed by me. I'm the founder, strategist, and editor — AI is the hands-on builder. I think that's a fair and increasingly normal way to describe how modern solo-founder products actually get made, and I'd rather say so clearly than let anyone assume otherwise.
