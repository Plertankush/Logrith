Getting the right number, not just a number

A calculator is only as good as the assumptions behind it. A BMI figure ignores muscle mass, a loan estimate depends on compounding frequency, and a currency conversion depends on which rate and which moment you use. These guides explain the formulas and the assumptions so you can judge when a result fits your situation.

What is covered

  • Health and body metrics: what BMI and body-fat estimates measure and where they mislead.
  • Finance: compound interest, loan affordability and percentage-based calculations, with worked examples.
  • Dates and time: exact age and date differences, including leap years and month-length edge cases.
  • Units and currency: converting between systems and understanding rate sources.

A note on accuracy

Results are estimates for planning and learning. For medical, tax or legal decisions, check the numbers with a qualified professional. Each guide lists the formula used, so you can verify any result by hand.
