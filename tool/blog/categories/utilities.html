Everyday tools, explained properly

Utilities are the tools people reach for in the middle of something else: generating a strong password, making a QR code for a menu, checking the time in another city, or splitting a group fairly. Because they are used quickly, the details are easy to get wrong. These guides slow down just enough to explain the details that matter.

What you will find

  • Security basics: why length beats complexity in passwords, and when a passphrase is the better choice.
  • Codes and labels: how QR codes and barcodes encode data, which format suits which use, and why a code that scans on screen can fail in print.
  • Time and randomness: how world clocks handle daylight saving, and what "random" really means in a coin flip, dice roll or team draw.
  • Money and units: comparing unit prices, working out payment fees, and converting file sizes without mixing up MB and MiB.

How to use these guides

Each guide begins with the quick answer, then covers edge cases, troubleshooting and the most common mistakes. Open the matching tool in another tab and try the examples as you read. Every tool runs in your browser, so nothing you type is uploaded.
