Branding, Sales & Growth

Positioning, brand identity, simple sales processes and growth loops that help a small business win and keep customers.

By Plert Ankush

Overview

A brand is the set of expectations people have of you. Clear positioning, a consistent look and honest communication make those expectations easy to form and easy to meet. For a small business, good branding is mostly clarity: who you serve, what you do better and why that matters.

Sales and growth turn attention into revenue. Most small businesses do better with a simple, repeatable process than with elaborate funnels, and they grow fastest when customers come back and recommend them.

What this section covers

  • Positioning: who you are for, and who you are not for
  • Visual identity basics: colour, type, logo and consistency
  • Writing a clear value proposition
  • A simple sales process: lead, conversation, proposal, follow-up
  • Pricing conversations and handling objections
  • Growth loops: referrals, content, product-led sharing

Core concepts

Positioning

The specific place your product holds in a customer's mind compared with the alternatives.

Value proposition

A short statement of the outcome you deliver and why it is better than other options.

Conversion rate

The share of people at one stage, such as visitors, who take the next step, such as signing up.

Growth loop

A cycle where each customer or piece of output helps bring in the next, such as sharing a result with a visible link.

Where to start

Write one sentence that finishes "We help ___ do ___ so they can ___." Test it with five customers. If they cannot repeat it back, simplify it before you design anything.
