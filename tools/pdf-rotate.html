Frequently Asked Questions

How do I rotate a PDF page?
Upload your PDF, select the rotation angle (90°, 180° or 270°), choose whether to apply to all pages or specific ones, then click Rotate PDF. Download the result instantly.
Can I rotate only certain pages?
Yes! Select "Specific Page Range" and enter pages like "1-3, 5". Or choose "Odd Pages Only" or "Even Pages Only" for quick batch selection.
Will rotating reduce PDF quality?
No. PDF-Lib rotates pages by modifying page metadata — no re-encoding occurs. The content quality is perfectly preserved.
Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?
No, the entire rotation process runs in your browser. Your document is never sent to a server, so it stays private throughout.
Can I rotate pages in different directions within the same PDF?
For a single operation, the chosen rotation angle applies to all selected pages. If different pages need different rotations, run the tool again on the result with a new page selection.
What causes PDF pages to be sideways in the first place?
This is usually caused by how a document was scanned, how a mobile phone was oriented when creating a PDF from a photo, or a page that was originally designed in landscape being inserted into a portrait document.

About the PDF Rotate Tool

The Logrith PDF Rotate tool fixes sideways or upside-down pages in a PDF document without needing to reprint, rescan, or install desktop software. Rotate individual pages, a custom range, odd or even pages only, or the entire document, all directly in your browser. This is one of the most common fixes needed after scanning paper documents, since scanners and multi-function printers frequently produce pages that are rotated 90 or 180 degrees from how they should appear.

A sideways PDF page is a small but genuinely annoying problem — the content is all there, correctly scanned and readable, but viewing or printing it means constantly tilting your head or your printed page. Rather than rescanning the document correctly, which isn't always possible if you no longer have the original paper, rotating the existing PDF pages fixes the orientation in seconds.

Flexible Rotation Options

  • Rotate all pages — apply the same rotation angle uniformly across the entire document, useful when an entire scan came out sideways.
  • Rotate a specific page range — enter page numbers or ranges, like "1-3, 5", to rotate only the pages that need it while leaving the rest untouched.
  • Rotate odd pages only — a common fix for double-sided scans where every other page came out rotated due to how the paper fed through the scanner.
  • Rotate even pages only — the counterpart fix for the opposite scanning pattern.

Why Scanned Documents Often Come Out Rotated

Flatbed scanners and automatic document feeders don't always know which way is "up" for a given page — they simply capture whatever orientation the paper was fed in. With double-sided scanning in particular, it's common for one side of each sheet to scan correctly while the other comes out rotated 180 degrees, because the paper physically flips between passes. This is exactly why the odd-pages-only and even-pages-only rotation options exist in this tool — they let you correct just the affected half of a double-sided scan without touching the pages that are already correct.

How Rotation Preserves Quality

This tool uses PDF-Lib, a library that modifies a PDF's internal page rotation metadata rather than converting pages into images and rendering them back into the document. Every PDF page has a rotation property as part of its definition, and changing that property simply tells PDF viewers to display the page at a different angle — the underlying text, vector graphics, and embedded images are untouched. This means rotating a PDF with this tool never reduces sharpness, never re-compresses embedded images, and never affects text selectability, unlike approaches that convert pages to images first.

Privacy and Browser-Based Processing

All rotation happens locally in your browser using JavaScript — your PDF is never uploaded to a server, stored, or logged. This makes the tool suitable even for sensitive documents like signed contracts, medical records, or financial statements that you'd rather not send to a third-party service just to fix their orientation.

Choosing the Right Rotation Angle

If you're unsure how many degrees a page needs to rotate, a quick way to check is to look at which direction the text currently reads. If the text runs from bottom to top when held normally, a 90° rotation in one direction will usually fix it. If it runs top to bottom, rotate the other way. If the page is simply upside down with text reading right-side-up but inverted, 180° is what you need. Since the tool shows results instantly, you can also just try a rotation and undo by re-uploading if it's not quite right, rather than needing to calculate the correct angle in advance.

Rotating vs. Other PDF Fixes

It helps to know when rotation is the right fix and when a different tool is needed. If the content itself is correct but just facing the wrong way, rotation is exactly what you want — it changes the display angle without touching anything else about the page. If instead you need to remove pages entirely, Logrith's PDF Page Remover is the right tool. If you need to combine several rotated and non-rotated documents into one, rotate the individual pages first, then use PDF Merge to combine them, since merging preserves each page's existing rotation setting.
