Where Lorem Ipsum Text Comes From

The passage traces back to a work of Latin philosophy — specifically, sections from Cicero's "de Finibus Bonorum et Malorum," written around 45 BC. Sometime in the 1500s, a printer scrambled a passage from this text to create a type specimen book, a sample sheet used to show off different typefaces and layouts. The scrambling removed any real meaning from the passage but kept its Latin-like appearance and natural rhythm. That scrambled version has been reused and slightly altered ever since, becoming the standard "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet..." text recognized across the publishing and design world today.

Why Designers Use Placeholder Text Instead of Real Content

  • Keeps focus on layout, not content. When reviewing a design mockup, readable text can distract viewers into reading and evaluating the words rather than the visual structure, spacing, and typography.
  • Mimics natural text patterns. Unlike repeating a single filler word like "text text text," Lorem Ipsum has varied word and sentence lengths that behave like real prose when flowing through a layout, revealing how text will actually wrap and break.
  • Universally recognized. Designers, developers, and clients across the industry recognize Lorem Ipsum as placeholder text immediately, so there's no confusion about whether it's meant to be final copy.
  • Available before real copy is ready. Design and development work often starts before final written content is finished — placeholder text lets layout work proceed in parallel rather than waiting.
